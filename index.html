How the expected range is made

Each day is compared with a forecast made before that day (exponential smoothing with weekly seasonality, refitted weekly). Known calendar events are left out. An issue opens when revenue, orders, visits or conversion leaves its range for long enough; see the accuracy page for how often that is right.

How this is calculated

Sales from tracked visits, plus sales whose visit started the day before, plus orders we could not link to a visit, minus discounts and refunds. The lines add up exactly to net revenue.

Daily trends

Recent refunds are restated as they arrive, so the latest days may understate refunds.
