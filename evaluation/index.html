Can a misleading summary slip through?

Data: Synthetic

The checker caught 78% of misleading summaries (352 of 450) that had correct numbers.

Summaries come from a template. A local model may only reword the short summary and pick next steps from a fixed list; if its text fails the checks twice, the template is used.

How it was tested

Each store is generated with planted changes whose answers are kept where the system cannot read them. Settings were tuned on a separate dev set; the held-out stores use different segments, smaller changes and one kind of change never seen in tuning, so their numbers are the honest ones.
